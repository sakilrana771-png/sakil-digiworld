About Us

Serving the people of Jalpaiguri & Kadobari Haat

Sakil DigiWorld is a local Digital Seva / Online Service Centre located at Sanpukuri Para, Kadobari Haat, Jalpaiguri. We help our neighbours — students, families, farmers, shopkeepers and senior citizens — with everyday online and government-related work.

From Aadhaar and PAN assistance to government scheme forms, banking help, printing, passport photos and ticket booking, we make digital services simple, accessible and friendly. We believe technology should help everyone — not be a barrier. That's why we patiently guide each customer, in a language they understand, with a smile.

We are an independent local digital service centre, not a government office or bank — but we make it easier for you to complete your work with the official authorities.

FAQ

Common Questions

Some helpful answers before you visit.

Is Sakil DigiWorld a government office?
No. We are an independent local digital service centre. We assist you in completing online and government-related services, but we are not an official government office, bank, Aadhaar centre or railway office.
What documents should I bring?
For Aadhaar or PAN work, please bring your existing Aadhaar/PAN card and the registered mobile number. For other services, call ahead on +91 86176 45792 and we'll tell you exactly what to bring.
Do you charge for your assistance?
Yes, we charge a small service fee for the assistance, time and convenience we provide. Any official government or portal fees are separate and paid directly to the concerned authority. Feel free to ask for a quote before we begin.
Can I book tickets over the phone?
Yes, you can WhatsApp or call us with your travel details. We'll help you check availability and book railway, bus or flight tickets as per your requirement.
